# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

The short version: be kind, assume good intent, prefer asking over assuming, no harassment.

Report conduct concerns to **conduct@chipid.vn**. Reports are confidential and handled by someone outside the reporter's chain of responsibility.
